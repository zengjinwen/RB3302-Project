to a rear pursuit target; Stanley reduces translation with heading error; DWA

All four modes now share single occupied-grid-cell detection and A* replanning.
A confirmed new cell replaces the old cell. Mapped wall returns are excluded.
A blocked goal is never skipped; no route waits. Four fresh clear observations
can release a removed obstacle; there is no blind expiry.
